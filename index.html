Cutting allowances Included in cloth planning

For a ½″ seam with aligned edges, add ½″ to each panel. Adding 0″ front / 1″ back and then sewing ½″ in transfers another ½″ of finished width to the back, changing the finished 45/55 split. Cloth planning uses allowance envelopes; check the original guide’s manual shaping separately.
